Overview

  • Abstract: This bundle contains the reflectance spectra of Ceres from 121 nm to 198 nm based on the slitless spectrographic observations using HST/ACS SBC performed on 2007 November 25. Ceres was spatially resolved to about 21 pixels in diameter.
